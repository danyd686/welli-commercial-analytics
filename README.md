# Welli | Commercial Analytics Case Study

## Project Overview

Commercial analytics project focused on evaluating Welli's partner network, identifying growth opportunities, prioritizing commercial partners, and designing a data-driven operating strategy.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze the commercial funnel, activation, and partner performance.
- Identify growth opportunities by sector, acquisition channel, and partner size.
- Define partner performance criteria and develop an ally scoring model.
- Design a lead scoring framework using available prospect information.
- Propose a commercial operating model for acquisition, onboarding, development, and reactivation.
- Use SQL to rank commercial segments and classify inactive locations.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Python:** Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib.
- **SQL:** SQLite, CTEs, window functions, aggregations, and ranking.
- **Environment:** Jupyter Notebook.
- **Presentation:** Google Slides.

## Key Findings

- 330 locations and 184 partners analyzed.
- 68.18% location activation rate.
- 31.82% of locations remain inactive.
- 80% of disbursements are concentrated in 21.21% of locations.
- Strategic partnerships show stronger conversion and lower delinquency than the Hunter channel.
- Aesthetic medicine presents an attractive combination of activation, conversion, and portfolio quality.

## Proposed Business Strategy

- Prioritize partners based on activation, productivity, conversion, portfolio quality, and network size.
- Improve onboarding and activation of existing locations.
- Distribute responsibilities across Hunters, Customer Success, and Farmers.
- Implement inactivity alerts, service-level agreements, and reactivation workflows.
- Validate activation initiatives through a controlled pilot.

## Methodological Notes

This case study uses simulated and confidential business data provided for assessment purposes. The original dataset is not included in this repository.

Findings are descriptive and should not be interpreted as causal relationships or predictive results. Proposed targets and operational recommendations require validation through implementation and experimentation.
